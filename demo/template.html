Interview Signals

An interviewer-side signal layer for mock interviews. Transcript goes in; a handful of typed, bounded decisions come out — phase, whether the last question was answered, clarity, rambling. Built on TypeSafe’s Jev, which returns labels and probabilities rather than prose, so software can act on them without parsing text.

The suppressed column is the point. Jev answers every question it is asked. Deciding which answers are worth an interviewer’s attention mid-conversation is a separate layer, and it discards most of them — for being below threshold, unchanged since last tick, too low-confidence, or simply too soon after the last card. A technically correct signal can still be bad product behaviour.

Questions that fail a code-side precondition are never sent at all: no outstanding question to judge, too little candidate speech, turn too short to ramble. Duration and counting logic stays in code, because Jev 1.13 does not count reliably.
